> *"I... I was just passing through. Through the time cracks. You know. Normal stuff. Ha ha. So... what's everyone doing?"*

He looked at the sword (Shelby). He looked at the sleeping Finn. He looked at BMO's smug face.

> *"Is this a bad time? I can come back. I can just... leave. Go back to my temporal hedge. It's fine. Everything's fine."*

Prismo waved him over:

> *"Nonsense, Fern! Join the temporal chaos! We're in PART 2 now!"*

Fern gulped:

> *"Part... 2? There's a Part 2? Oh no, I missed Part 1, didn't I? I'm always missing things! The timeline is SCARY!"*

> *"Why is there a SWORD in the room? And a BUSH? And why is PRISCO here?! Wait— HOW long was I ASLEEP?!"*

BMO pointed at the sleeping-Finn drawing on Finn's face:

## 👩 SCENE 9: Finn's Mom in the Reflection

The TV screen shifted... and revealed **FINN'S MOM!**

> *"Finn! Did you clean your room?! And finish your commits?! And check the PROVISION status like I TOLD you to?!"*

> *"M-MOM?! You're... in the TV?!"*

*The timeline shuddered...*

Finn blinked. Looked around. Everything seemed... normal. But the air felt thicker. The clocks ticked forward again, but one of them—the ancient grandfather clock in the corner—still ticked backward.

> *"Was... was that real?"* Finn whispered.

Prismo smiled mysteriously:

> *"The timeline you just experienced... wasn't THIS one."*

> *"It was a fever dream,"* Prisco added. *"A multiverse hiccup. A timeline crossover."*

> *"Or maybe you're just still dreaming,"* BMO offered.

Finn rubbed his eyes:

> *"I... don't know what happened. But I'm awake now. And I think I need another nap."*
