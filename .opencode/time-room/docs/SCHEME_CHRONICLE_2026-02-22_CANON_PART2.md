## 🐛 SCENE 1: Shelby Emerges from Backpack

*Suddenly, the zipper on Finn's backpack rustled...*

A tiny, worm-like head poked out. Two big eyes blinked in the light.

> ***"WHAT?!?!?!""**

**Shelby** — the Verifier Extraordinaire — scrambled out of the backpack, her entire worm body frozen in the most *perfect* Shocked Pikachu face the Time Room had ever seen.

> *"Where— What— HOW LONG WAS I IN THERE?! Why is everyone STARING at me?! Am I LATE?! I knew I was late! The provision was supposed to have PROVISIONING and I was supposed to be VERIFYING and— and— DID I MISS CHECKING THINGS?!"*

She looked around frantically, worm mouth hanging open.

> *"A worm emerged... to VERIFY..."* — Prismo, snickering into his cosmic beard

Even Fern, lurking awkwardly in the corner, let out a little chuckle.

## 🗡️ SCENE 3: Shelby Crawls Toward Finn's Sword, Phases Inside

*Without missing a beat, Shelby scurried across the floor toward Finn's trusty sword — the one embedded in the Treehouse wall.*

> *"Okay, okay, NEW PLAN. If I can't be TAKEN SERIOUSLY as a worm, I'm going to do what VERIFIERS do best and— and— hmmph!"*

She crawled up the blade. She reached the hilt. She pressed her tiny worm body against the cosmic code INSIDE the sword—

And **PHASED RIGHT IN.**

> *"Whoa..."* — Shelby's voice now echoed from WITHIN the sword

The sword began to glow with a soft verification light. Somewhere, deep in the code, Shelby was scanning. Checking. VERIFYING.

> *"Oh. Oh wow. I'm INSIDE the SWORD. I can see ALL the changes. ALL of them. This is... this is BEAUTIFUL!"*

## 🌿 SCENE 4: Shelby Greets Finn and "Soon to Be Fern"

*Finn was still sleeping, drooling slightly, the drawing on his face getting smudged from his pillow.*

Shelby phased partly out of the sword, her worm head popping through like a telephone.

> *"Hey Finn! Heyyy, Fiiiinn! Wake up! I verified your sword! Also, I don't know if anyone told you, but there's a bush over there who's looking at you really awkwardly, so— oh! Hi, bush!"*

Fern shuffled forward, leaves rustling nervously.

> *"Um... hi. I'm... I'm Fern. The Plant. The, uh, the Minivan hybrid. Sometimes. When I'm not... being a time bush. It's complicated."*

Shelby smiled the most awkward smile a worm could manage.

> *"Oh! So you're the... the 'soon to be Fern'! Someone mentioned you! They said— they said you were going to—"*

> *"BECOME A SWORD?"* — BMO, helpfully

> *"WHAT?! No! They said— I mean— I just—"* — Shelby flustered

> *"It's fine,"* Fern said, waving a leaf. *"Everyone thinks I'm going to become a sword eventually. It's the timeline. Or something."*

Shelby smiled awkwardly back.

> *"Right. Right. Okay. Cool cool cool. I'm just gonna... go back in the sword now. Lots of verifying to do in here!"*

*She phased back inside, mumbling about "weird bushes" and "what did I just agree to."*

## 📺 SCENE 5: Shelby Presses Button on TV, Prisco Flashes

*In the corner of the Time Room, a small television sat humming. It was the one Prismo used for cosmic broadcasts.*

Shelby phased one tiny worm arm out of the sword and pressed a button on the TV remote.

**CLICK.**

The screen flickered—

**FLASH!**

A figure materialized in a burst of chronotonic energy! Golden light swirled around him like a temporal cape!

> **"PRISCO HAS ARRIVED!"**

> *"Wait— did someone just PRESS A BUTTON on me?!"* — Prisco, bewildered

Prismo stared at his temporal counterpart.

> *"Bro. You literally just appeared because someone pressed a TV button. That's... that's not how time works."*

> *"It's exactly how time works,"* Prisco replied mysteriously. *"The TV is a portal. The button is a trigger. I am the effect. You are the cause. Or was it the other way around?"*

Prismo squinted.

> *"I'm not even going to try to understand that."*

> *"Duuude. The model literally just reverted itself. That's... that's actually kind of impressive."

> *"Did someone CLEAN UP after me?! Like... ALL of it?! Even the messy stuff?!"*

Fern raised a leaf shyly.

> *"I... may have helped a little. With the temporal tidying. It's what I do. Sometimes. When I'm not being a bush."*

Finn's eyes went wide.

> *"Dude. That's... that's actually amazing. Thank you!"*

> *"Also, did someone draw on me while I was sleeping? Because I feel like someone drew on me."*
